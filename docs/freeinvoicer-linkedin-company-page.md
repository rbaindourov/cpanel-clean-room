## About / Description (2,000 char limit)

FreeInvoicer builds software that takes the manual work out of invoicing and accounts payable.

For finance teams, our AI Accounts Payable Agent ingests supplier invoices, matches them against purchase orders and receiving records, checks for duplicate billing and anomalies, and syncs approved bills to your accounting system. When something doesn't add up, the agent flags it and routes it to a person with suggested next steps. Every action is logged in an audit trail with its rationale.

For freelancers, contractors, and small businesses, FreeInvoicer Studio is a free invoice builder that produces clean single-page US Letter PDFs, with JSON export. A free desktop app is available for Windows, macOS, and Linux.

What we offer:
- Smart invoice ingestion and OCR extraction
- 3-way purchase order matching
- Fraud and anomaly detection
- ERP integrations (QuickBooks Online, Xero, NetSuite, Sage, SAP)
- Human-in-the-loop review for exceptions
- Vendor payments by ACH and virtual card
- e-Invoicing tooling, including Peppol BIS 3.0 / UBL 2.1 XML generation
- Free invoice templates and plain-language invoicing guides

Pricing is simple: the Studio is free, and the Autonomous AP Agent is $20/month.

Try it: https://freeinvoicer.com
